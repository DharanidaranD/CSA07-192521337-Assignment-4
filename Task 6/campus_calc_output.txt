== Collapsed core vs three-tier (assumed costs, Rs) ==
three-tier: 2 core + 6 distribution = 8 devices, cost devices Rs 3,900,000; fibre runs core-dist 12 + dist-access 48
collapsed : 2 core = 2 devices (saves 6), device cost Rs 1,800,000; saves Rs 2,100,000 but needs longer runs/ports on core: 48 runs of core ports
net device+port+fibre saving = Rs 3,252,000 (links removed: 12)
If a campus-wide outage costs Rs 150,000/hour (assumed), break-even = 14.0 outage-hours in 10 years
Single core failure: collapsed core with ONE core = whole campus down; with a pair, it survives but all load and all
risk sit on 2 boxes; three-tier confines a distribution failure to its zone (hostels, labs, ...).
